## Opening hours

The staff cafeteria opens at 7:30 a.m. and closes at 3:00 p.m. on weekdays.
On Saturdays it is open from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Payment

Only cards and the staff badge wallet are accepted. Cash is not accepted.

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Coffee | $1.50 |
| Lunch special | $6.75 |
| Fruit cup | $2.25 |

## Feedback

Send suggestions to the facilities team. Menu changes are announced every Monday.
